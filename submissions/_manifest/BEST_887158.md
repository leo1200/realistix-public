## Rebuilding it

Two routes, and they answer different questions.

* **From the archived checkpoints** — `archive/887158/source_runs/` holds the
  four `best.pt` files the zip was packed from, so the artefact can be rebuilt
  without a GPU. This is the route that survives losing the runs directory.

  2026-08-18 on an h200 against the h100-built original: 0 of 80 float tensors
  identical, and the two score within **0.004 of final**. This is the route the
  Decision Phase takes, and the one that has to keep working.
