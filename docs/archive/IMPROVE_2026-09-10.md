# Improvement screens of 2026-09-10 — stochastic regularisation, sim/real SPS split, time dilation

Joint Claude (Fable 5.1) + Codex (gpt-6-astra) round following `docs/AUDIT_2026-09-10.md`. The owner asked to build
and evaluate the audit's proposals plus two ideas of their own: **time dilation** (train on windows read at
double speed so the model must infer the evolution clock from the input — Reynolds number as a time scaling)
and **different loss treatment of simulation and real data**. Everything here: 33 training runs (seed 0 on
three geometries for seven arms; seed 1 for the controls and one arm; one dose extension), ~15 A100-hours,
step 6000 of the shipped 8000-step cosine, `--stop-at 6000`. Artefacts: `research/improve_2026-09-10/`
(Claude: launcher, readers, per-run cell JSON, NOTES.md; Codex: briefs 3–5, reports 3–5, probes).

## Verdict

**No arm clears its pre-registered gate; the shipped recipe (918211's members) stays.** Three results are
worth keeping:

1. **Both tested changes to the simulation SPS-bulk term hurt at the held-out angle (seed 0).** Removing
   simulation from `sps_bulk`'s numerator (R) or pricing its errors in real-like units (U, sim scale × 0.18)
   both cost ≈ 1.5 % relative-L2 and ≈ 3.5 % MVPE at angle05 (−0.030 / −0.023 direct). The audit's "wrong
   units" finding stands (sim carries 87.5 % of the term's gradient in non-dimensional units), and the result
   supports keeping the baseline contribution under this recipe. One angle and one seed do not establish a
   transfer mechanism or close every sim/real loss treatment; U changes residual selectivity as well as
   strength, and up-weighting was not tested. No further funding for removing or softening sim SPS.
2. **Time dilation at p = 0.25, factor 2, real windows only, was a fair test of this dose and mechanics and does
   not pay.** Relative-L2 improves 0.2 % at angle05 and 0.6 % at far-Re (0.9 % on the farthest row) but rises
   0.4 % on mim1, and TKE and MVPE pay at the far-Re row (3 steps past the training edge) where the mechanism was
   supposed to help: −0.012 mim1, +0.009 angle05, −0.008 far. Telemetry: 25.17 % of real draws dilated, every training trajectory exposed, no fallbacks;
   37 of 71 far-training trajectories had stride-2 frame correlations below the slowest native regime, so the
   intervention reached "faster than anything seen" (realised start histograms were not recorded, so start
   coverage is inferred, not measured). The closed 2026-09-01 variant (dilating toward a
   neighbouring Re to synthesise fields) and this one now fail for different reasons; Re is not a pure time
   scaling, and the augmentation removes a valid spatial-structure→clock cue (a plausible reading, not a
   demonstrated cause). Funding for this arm is closed.
3. **DropPath reads positive pooled folds and positive means in both seeds, below the bar.** With gradient clearing (Z) as its base:
   dose 0.03 → +0.005 / +0.007 / −0.021 (mim1 / angle05 / far, seed 0, vs B); **0.08 → +0.017 / +0.001 / +0.001
   (seed 0), −0.002 / +0.030 / +0.016 (seed 1), pooled +0.011, 5 of 6 pairs positive**; 0.15 → +0.004 / +0.020 /
   +0.005 (seed 0). Against Z: pooled +0.011. **D08 stops**: seed-1 means +0.0148 vs B and +0.0080 vs Z miss the
   frozen requirements (> +0.0236 and > +0.0150; angle vs Z +0.024 against ≥ +0.049), so the exploratory
   replication does not repair the seed-0 failure. **D15 stops**: mean vs B +0.009 ≤ +0.015 and angle vs Z
   +0.010 < +0.020. Mimic reverses between D08's seeds and angle vs Z reverses; D03 → D08 → D15 means vs Z rise
   but mimic peaks at 0.08 and angle is non-monotonic, so no dose-response or optimal dose is established, and
   every D effect is conditional on gradient clearing (a B + DropPath arm was not run). For scale, `wd 0.03`'s
   +0.018 fold read transferred to −0.008 of board accuracy — one precedent, not a calibration. Zero-rest: far-Re
   −0.037 on seed 0 and +0.016 on seed 1 (sign reversed between seeds; recurring harm unconfirmed); pooled −0.001
   / +0.008 / −0.010.

## Protocol (pre-registered in Codex round 3, `research/improve_2026-09-10/codex/codex_report3.md`)

Arms on `kfold.CURRENT_BASE` (= 918211's member recipe): **B** control · **Z** `--zero-rest` (every noise arm
inherits it) · **R** `--sps-domain real` (real-only numerator, whole-batch denominator) · **U** `--sim-sps-scale
0.18` · **T** `--time-dilate 0.25 --dilate-factors 2 --dilate-domain real` · **D03 / D08** `--zero-rest
--drop-path p` (branch drop on Block residuals, 0 / p/2 / p at widths 96 / 192 / 384, private RNG, off at eval)
· **D15** dose extension (exploratory). Folds: mim1 (interior cells), angle05 (angle withheld from real and sim),
far_drop (edge Re, ten cells). Read: `step6000.pt` scored on the held-out stride-40 windows with the kit's
per-window errors, averaged within cell, cells equally (far: cells → Re row → rows equally),
Q = .4738 S(rel) + .1057 S(tke) + .0926 S(mvpe). Gates: continue only if equal-fold mean > +0.015 and no fold
< −0.020; R/U every fold > 0; T far ≥ +0.020 and mimic ≥ −0.010; D angle ≥ +0.020 vs Z and mimic ≥ −0.010.

Implementation (`tools/train_mixed.py`, `models/unet.py`, `gpudata.py`, `losses.py`; patches in
`research/improve_2026-09-10/claude/`): all flags inert at their defaults — verified bit-identical against the
pre-change tree (history metrics and all 164 tensors of a 20-step checkpoint equal), and B reproduces
`bt_wd03mv_mim1_s0`'s step-6000 errors exactly. Codex's round-4 audit found and we fixed a dilation start
off-by-one, missing dilation telemetry and four launcher gaps before the arms were queued. New flags also
available for later screens: `--chan-drop`, `--frame-mask`, `--dilate-factors 0.5`, `--dilate-domain all`.

## What this licenses

* Keep `sps_bulk` as it is, including its simulation contribution. Do not fund sim-SPS removal/softening again.
* Time dilation as specified is closed; p = 0.5, real+sim, or factor 3 would be new hypotheses with a weak
  prior (the far row paid TKE/MVPE). Not funded.
* DropPath 0.08 (with `--zero-rest`) is the one lever with positive pooled folds on all three geometries, and
  it fails its frozen gates: leave it. These member screens carry no ensemble or learned-head SPS evidence, so a
  full-data D08 submission would bypass the failed gates and the ensemble/head confirmation; it could only be a
  separately authorised transfer experiment, and one submission would not establish transfer for the
  regularisation class. The thirteen-fold ensemble confirmation (≈ 40 runs) is not justified by +0.011.
* The audit's plan stands: recipe/package freeze first, the matched timing pair, meanfix as a calibration slot.


## Wave 2 and 3 (2026-09-10 night → 09-11): the owner's continuous sim/real fraction, frame masking, duration

**Continuous sim/real fraction — closes as specified; a narrow far-Re signal remains.** Implemented as mean-flow
mixing (`--mix-prob p --mix-alpha a --mix-cond alpha|real`): with probability p a real window's input and target
are shifted by α·(mean_sim(cell) − mean_real(cell)) on cells observed in every frame of both trajectories,
α ~ U(0, a); the modality FiLM either interpolates its real/sim rows at α (`_apply` float branch, exact at 0/1) or is
told nothing. Pointwise mixup of the unaligned fields was not built (fluctuations add in quadrature; the hybrid's
TKE is wrong by construction). A first set of runs carried a defect found by Codex (the shift reached
intermittently observed cells and turned ~2.7 % of exact-zero targets into fabricated targets); the corrected
reruns are the read (cell-aggregated dQ vs B, seed 0):

| arm | mim1 | angle05 | far_drop | mean | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MIX2b (α ≤ 1, FiLM sees α) | +0.002 | −0.013 | +0.011 | 0.000 | fails |
| MIX2rb (α ≤ 1, hidden) | +0.003 | −0.013 | +0.020 | +0.003 | far passes, mean fails |
| MIX2a (α ≤ 0.5, FiLM sees α) | +0.021 | −0.024 | +0.015 | +0.004 | fails (angle floor) |

All six mixing runs (defective and corrected) improve far_drop by +0.011…+0.030; the corrected ones lose ≈ 0.013–0.024
at the novel angle. Codex's decomposition: the far gain is relative-L2-led with a TKE cost at the 3-step row, and
the MVPE improvement (−2…−7 % at 3 steps) is ~90 % one cell (26700_15). Reading: shifting the mean flow by the
sim/real level difference buys some robustness to Reynolds-like level errors of the mean flow, not to the shape
changes a new angle brings, and the evidence is one seed and one cell for the MVPE part. Hiding α (the network
must infer the shifted mean) is better at far-Re than disclosing it (+0.010) and no worse elsewhere, in line with
the owner's "make the model infer it" intuition, but neither variant clears the gate.

**Input-frame masking (FM05) is dead**: −0.018 / −0.055 / −0.079; corrupting temporal evidence hurts as dilation did.

**Regularisation × duration is closed**: on a 12k cosine read at 9000, DropPath 0.08 reads +0.004 vs B and −0.002 vs
itself at 8k@6000; without DropPath the long schedule loses −0.025. Interaction +0.014 < the +0.020 gate; both 12k
curves peak near 8000 and decline.

**Timing: measured on the board, and closed.** Two probe submissions with our exact payload and one window per
call — P0 persistence (0.35 ms of GPU work on an A100-PCIE) and P1 identical outputs plus 3.2 ms of GPU work — read
**5.34 ms and 6.49 ms per sample** (921738 / 921760, 2026-09-10/11). Slope 0.36 board-ms per local GPU-ms and
intercept **F ≈ 5.2 ms**: the container's per-call cost is ≈ 5 ms independent of our kernels, and the incumbent's
5.25 ms implies ≈ 0.1 ms of visible GPU work against 3.9 ms measured locally (its chain overlaps the harness cost).
Saving a full millisecond of local GPU work is worth ≤ +0.03 of final; there is nothing to fuse. The record's F ≈ 3.4
was too low, the A100 proxy (1.3 ms) far too low. `submissions/_manifest/CANDIDATE_timing_probes.md`.

**Carry-forward (Codex round 9, agreed):** nothing measured in waves 1–3 goes to the thirteen-fold ensemble read.
The last bounded screen — LEVEL10, a scalar level perturbation of the real window's own mean flow (β ~ U(−0.1, 0.1),
no simulation involved) — read **+0.006 / −0.004 / −0.013 (mean −0.004)** and made far-Re *worse* (MVPE +2.4 %, the
3-step row −0.040): generic level variation does not reproduce the mixing arm's far-Re gain, so the CFD sim/real
mean-flow direction was the operative ingredient of the owner's idea, while the angle cost is what kept it below the
bar. The branch ends here under its pre-registered rule. What remains: package freeze, meanfix as a calibration slot, and the incumbent; the time channel is closed.
