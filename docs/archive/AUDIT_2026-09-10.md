## 0. Verdict in six lines

1. **The public board is converged for us.** Since 904950 (08-28) the final rose +0.171; **+0.158 is `time_score`**
   (8.02 → 5.25 ms/sample) and **+0.008 is accuracy** (rel+tke+mvpe+sps under the fitted weights), from thirteen
   submissions and roughly 250 GPU-hours of screening. Three accuracy candidates paid +0.020 / +0.008 / −0.007.
2. **No shipped bug is hiding score.** Losses reproduce `scoring.py` to float precision; EMA/cosine/bf16 placement
   is correct; no leakage; every 2026-09-09 knob is inert at its default. The known defects (never-zeroed AdamW
   group, in-sample head, hard output mask) are all measured at ≤ 0.02 and are not the lever.
3. **One new objective-design confound**: the `sps_bulk` term multiplies the error by the window's velocity scale
   before dividing by the real-data sigma, and simulation is non-dimensional (scale ≈ 1.0 vs ≈ 0.18 m/s for real). On
   twelve shipped-recipe batches, simulation supplied **87.5 % of the term's parameter-gradient norm** (97 % of its
   output-gradient energy). The term paid +0.057 on the board as it is; whether it pays more when it stops being a
   sim-shaping term in the wrong units is a cheap, unrun screen.
4. **The one untested generic lever is stochastic regularisation** (DropPath / channel dropout / input-frame
   masking): zero dropout modules exist in any of the 3,117 archived configurations, and regularisation is the only
   direction with a repeatable novel-regime signal (wd 0.03 +0.057 on angle folds; early stopping; weight-noise
   controls). Expected value is small on the board (≈ +0.005 [−0.05, +0.04]); the case is the Decision Phase.
5. **The largest operational risk is recipe drift**, not score: `tools/train_final.sh` with default settings rebuilds a
   904950-era model (no `--wd-muon`, `--w-mvpe 0`, `CHAMPION=initmove`); reproducing 918211 needs
   `W_MVPE=0.3 WD_MUON=0.03 CHAMPION=wd03mv`. `check_recipe`/`verify_best` still default to 904950. The organisers
   retrain shortlisted methods from scratch and disqualify what does not reproduce (`rules.md` Final Verification).
6. **Time is bounded but not measured.** The container's per-call intercept (≈ 3.3 ms) is inferred from two board
   points. A matched pair of trivial torch-CUDA submissions identifies it for our payload class in two slots; until
   then the remaining kernel work (≈ 0.2 ms on A100, ≈ +0.013) is not worth engineering days.

**Corrections to the record.**
* *Resolution.* The fold rig's seed noise at fixed step 6000 is **sd 0.009 per run** (4 folds × 4 seeds of
  `kf_tke1em8`), not 0.02–0.03; the arm × fold effect sd in the archive is ≈ 0.009 median (0.011 for wd03). The rig
  is precise; its problem is validity — transfer to the board (tke sign flips, mvpe under-priced 5×, ensemble
  compression) and generalisation from four reused ten-cell folds. More seeds buy nothing; more geometries do.
* *"Closed".* Of 97 paired arm families with ≥ 8 runs at fixed 6000, 41 read inside ±0.03 (Codex archive scan). A
  closure is a "not worth funding at +0.06 on this geometry" decision, not a refuted mechanism; the wd axis reopened
  outside its swept range and paid. The single positive unshipped arm in the whole archive is `kf_wd03` (+0.019) —
  already shipped.
* *Hidden-set arithmetic.* 7575_0 is a duplicate measurement, not a withheld cell; with 18 sim-only cells,
  (99−81)/2 = 9 is an integer. That repairs the counting argument; it does not establish membership or weights.
  Of the 18, 5 are the Re 27975 row (extrapolation), 5 the interior Re 15225 row, 4 the high-Re AoA-5 block, 4
  singletons; 11 of 18 need angle-specific Re extrapolation. Novel *intermediate* angles are the rules' example,
  not a released fact. Weight the far-Re and interior-Re geometries at least equally with the angle suite.
* *Weights.* The 14-point fit (LOO RMS 0.0115) holds on 34 cards (0.0096); a refit gives rel 0.462 / mvpe 0.111 and an
  intercept fit is ill-conditioned (κ ≈ 3·10⁵). Keep the locked weights; report sensitivity; do not read the fourth
  decimal.
* *Transfer coefficients* (mvpe 5×, tke 1.3×/3.1×, head 1.22 ± 0.32) remain anecdotes; the head coefficient missed
  40× on 911563. Use them for sign and order only — the record says this and then prices with them anyway.
* *Under-claimed:* time engineering, dismissed for three weeks, delivered 92 % of the month's gain.

## 2. Code and process audit — what is actually shipped

Verified (file:line in `research/audit_2026-09-10/claude/subagent_*.md` and Codex's reports): scorer-equivalent
losses (masking, per-window ratios, probe rake, TKE definition); EMA 0.998 after `opt.step`/`sched.step`, checkpoint
at 6000 of an 8000-step cosine (lr 14.6 % of peak, by design); bf16 autocast with an fp32 anchor promotion that keeps
the losses fp32; uniform (trajectory, start) sampling, 19 sim + 13 real per batch, each real start drawn 1.18× per
run; no leakage under the holdout env vars; the uncommitted 14-file diff adds only inert knobs. FiLM folds exactly
into GroupNorm at export, so the shipped member is a plain 22.65 M-parameter residual U-Net (dense 40→96 stem,
levels 96/192/384, GN(8)+SiLU, zero-init 96→40 head, last-frame anchor) with **no dropout or stochastic depth**.

Known and measured, not levers: never-zeroed AdamW group (`--zero-rest` +0.000 mimic / +0.020 angle); head fitted
in-sample (nested/OOF heads −0.02…−0.06 sps; λ* = 1.0); hard output mask at exact-zero cells (0.1 % of squared error);
sim share of the batch (`sim_batch_frac` 0.25: −0.010); coop/meanfix (TKE-funded, rel/mvpe/sps pay).

**New: the `sps_bulk` unit confound.** `losses.sps_bulk` computes `exp(−|e|·scale/σ)` with σ = 0.0564 m/s
(`losses.py:243`); `gpudata` restores each modality's native scale (`gpudata.py:112`). Real windows have scale
≈ 0.18, simulation ≈ 1.0, so a simulated error is priced 5.6× harsher in a unit that is not m/s. Measured on
`wd03mv_m0` at EMA 6000 over twelve exact batches: sim carries 97.3 % of the output-gradient energy and
**87.5 % [80.4, 91.4] of the parameter-gradient norm** of the term; per-element reward 0.93 real vs 0.80 sim
(`research/audit_2026-09-10/codex/round2_sps.json`). The term's board value (+0.057, 901812) therefore came mostly
from shaping the members on simulation in wrong units. Screen (below) deletes only the sim contribution while
keeping the global denominator and every other loss term unchanged.

**Hazards for the Decision Phase.**
* Recipe drift in `train_final.sh` / `check_recipe` / `verify_best` / `REPRODUCE.md` (above). The script also inherits
  split and sim-directory environment variables from the caller.
* Import-time setup (load + three eager warm-ups + nine graph captures, 7.5 s measured) is budgeted only between
  captures; the stage wall is 180 s and the Decision-Phase harness's subprocess structure is unknown. Capture N = 1
  first; put one deadline around all of setup; verify against the organisers' promised evaluation example.
* `SubmissionModel(device="cpu")` ignores its argument on a CUDA host (`submission.py:295`); CPU execution works only
  when CUDA is absent. Honour the argument.
* `measured_channels` makes the scored channel set target-dependent; a private target with non-zero pressure would
  penalise our hard-zeroed p. Low probability; note it.

## 3. Where the remaining gains are

**Public board (to 09-20):** accuracy ≤ +0.05 at any transfer the record has ever shown; time ≈ +0.013 from
measured inference simplifications (0.17 ms H100 → ≈ 0.2 ms A100), more only if the per-call intercept is smaller
than the two-point inference says. The shortlist margin (0.338) is comfortable; #4 is 0.0003 behind, so rank 3 is not.

**Decision Phase (private set):** the only place a lever several times the bar exists. Whole-angle holdout costs
+16–20 % rel_l2 (6–9.5 % real-coverage-specific, growing after step 4000); edge-Re extrapolation costs mvpe fastest.
Every angle mechanism (morph, geo-in, temporal stem, early stop) bought 1–2.5 % rel_l2 per member and was paid back
at ensemble level through fluctuation amplitude at 5°. Codex's amplitude probe (seeds 3/4/5, nine geometries, 2,271
windows) shows a **target-informed scalar gain recovers 0.000088** direct points at 5° — the scalar-amplitude rescue
is dead; what survives is a spatial fluctuation-pattern question (84–94 % of squared TKE error is pattern after a
per-window scalar projection), which is a new hypothesis, not an overlooked knob.

Frontier facts that bound everything: TKE is 89–94 % pattern; the ensemble mean's TKE deficit is the disagreement
identity; the head sits at 90–97 % of its reachable interval gap; slow-mode skill is the same in- and out-of-sample.
No measured lever moves skill itself.

**4a. Regularisation protocol (pre-registered).** All arms keep `wd 0.03, w_mvpe 0.3, EMA 0.998, batch 32, 8000-step
cosine, EMA read at 6000`, no checkpoint or tke re-selection. Controls: **B** = unchanged recipe and **Z** = B +
`--zero-rest` (every "machinery" arm inherits ≈ +0.02 on angles from gradient clearing; noise arms must beat Z and B).
Arms: DropPath on `Block`'s residual branch after `c2`, inverted scaling, max p 0.03 / 0.08 at width 384, half at 192,
zero at 96; channel dropout 0.03 / 0.08 after the second SiLU in width-384 blocks only; input-frame masking 0.05 / 0.10
on frames 1–19 (both velocity channels, anchor frame kept, normalisation statistics unchanged). Separate noise RNG so
sampling stays paired; p = 0 must reproduce the control bit-for-bit; noise off at evaluation. Prediction: DropPath
0.03 is the likeliest modest help; stronger noise over-regularises matrices wd already shrank 40–60 %.
Dose screen: 8 arms × {mim1, angle05-drop} × seed 0 = 16 runs; pick ≤ 1 arm by its worse-fold gain over Z; continue
only if angle ≥ +0.02, mimic ≥ −0.01. Then B/Z/winner × {mim1–4, angle05/10-drop, far_drop/keep} × seeds 0/1 = 48 runs;
kill if angle mean < +0.02 vs Z, mimic mean < 0, or any fold < −0.02 vs B. Confirmation: fresh member seeds 11/12/13,
three-member ensembles on all thirteen folds of §4b, heads fitted on outer-training real trajectories only, read the
fp16 ensemble with learned bounds; require the §4b gate against B and a positive increment over Z.

**4b. Minimax suite and promotion rule (lock now, use for anything before 09-20).** Thirteen fold manifests: mim1–4
(as historically); angle05/10/15 each with target-angle sim retained and removed (explicit modality manifests —
`SIM_KEEPS_CELLS` does not override an angle holdout); far_drop / far_keep (their actual ten-cell holdout); I12675
(all five real cells at Re 12675 withheld, sim retained — the interior-interpolation proxy, since Re 15225 has no
real targets). Score non-overlapping stride-40 windows with the kit; equal trajectory/cell weight within folds; far
cells averaged within each Re distance, then equally over the three. Family weights M/A/E/I = ¼ each (decision
weights, not inferred hidden frequencies). Non-time weights locked at (0.4738, 0.2529, 0.1057, 0.0926); SPS omitted
only in member screens. **Promote only if** full weighted gain ≥ +0.04, every family ≥ 0, every fold ≥ −0.02, the
verdict survives weight sensitivity and independently seeded head refits, timing stays in budget and the frozen
package reproduces. Otherwise keep B. The public board is a guard and calibration, not a dose-selection loop.

## 7. The eight working hypotheses, settled

H1 board exhausted — a *practical* stopping rule, not a proof of optimality. H2 closures — resolved on the mimic
geometry (seed sd 0.009), unestablished on transfer; "closed" means "not worth +0.06 on these folds". H3
regularisation — the one unscreened family; its supporting reads (SAM-random +0.029, meta-random +0.047) are
confounded with gradient clearing (+0.02), hence the Z control. H4 ensemble amplitude coupling — confirmed; scalar
rescue dead (0.000088). H5 TKE — a frontier trade in every measured intervention; 84–94 % pattern. H6 geometry —
unknown; use the minimax suite; do not aim at angles alone. H7 retrain stability — plausible, unmeasured for the
current recipe; the hazard is drift, which is fixable. H8 slots — cheap for measurements, never free of preparation.
